COMPLIANCE HANDBOOK 2024
SECTION 3: FILING OBLIGATIONS
All registered entities must submit annual documentation before the
statutory deadline set out by the supervising authority each year.
Article 7(a) requires that submissions be made in electronic form.
Article 7(b) states that late filing incurs a penalty of 250 euros per
month of delay, capped at 3000 euros in any single reporting period.
Article 7(c) permits an extension of up to 30 days where the entity can
demonstrate exceptional circumstances beyond its reasonable control.
SECTION 4: SANCTIONS AND APPEALS
Fines and sanctions are applied progressively depending on the severity
and duration of the breach identified during review.
The appeals committee reviews challenges to sanctions on a quarterly
basis and must issue a written determination within 60 days.
Repeat breaches within a 24 month window attract a multiplier of 1.5
applied to the base penalty amount.
SECTION 5: RECORD RETENTION
Entities must retain supporting records for a minimum of seven years
from the end of the reporting period to which they relate.
SECTION 6: REGISTRATION
New entities must register within 90 days of commencing regulated activity.
Registration requires a designated compliance officer to be named.
The registration fee is 500 euros and is non-refundable once processed.
SECTION 7: REPORTING THRESHOLDS
Transactions exceeding 10000 euros must be reported individually.
Aggregated transactions from a single counterparty exceeding 50000 euros
in any calendar quarter must also be reported.
Article 12(a) requires reports to be filed within five working days.
Article 12(b) sets out the format and required data fields for reports.
SECTION 8: INSPECTIONS
The supervising authority may conduct an inspection with 14 days notice.
Unannounced inspections are permitted where fraud is reasonably suspected.
Entities must provide access to all systems and records during inspection.
Obstruction of an inspection carries a penalty of 5000 euros.
